Sense · Analyze · Prioritize · Respond

Four steps between a reading and a response.

The grid never just reports numbers. Every packet is scored, ranked against every other room on campus, and turned into something a person can act on.

STEP 01

Sense

An ESP32 in each lab samples gas, flame, heat, humidity, water and motion, then publishes one consolidated JSON packet over MQTT.

STEP 02

Analyze

Every reading is checked against a configurable threshold band and folded into a rolling 60-sample history, so trends show up before limits do.

STEP 03

Prioritize

Each room inherits its worst active metric. A flame reading outranks a humid afternoon, so the room that matters most rises to the top of the board.

STEP 04

Respond

The card flashes, a toast names the room and the cause, and the alert lands in a timestamped stream you can review long after the moment has passed.

Sensor coverage

Seven ways a room can tell you something is wrong.

Each laboratory carries the same instrument set, so a reading from the Server Room means exactly what it means in the IoT Lab.

Flame

IR flame detector · ADC

Reads inverted — the value falls as fire approaches. Crossing the low threshold is an instant critical, no averaging, no delay.

MQ-2 · Combustible gas

LPG · Propane · Smoke

The first thing to move during a solvent spill or a gas leak, usually well before anything is visible in the room.

MQ-7 · Carbon monoxide

CO · Odourless

Watches for the hazard nobody in the room can detect unaided. Sustained elevation escalates on its own schedule.

MQ-135 · Air quality

VOC · NH₃ · Benzene

The slow-moving one. Its trend line is usually the earliest sign that ventilation has quietly stopped doing its job.

Temperature & humidity

DHT · °C / %RH

Server rooms fail hot and equipment fails damp. Both are banded separately so a warm day never masks a cooling fault.

Water level

Analog probe · % + raw

Reported as both a percentage and the raw ADC count, so a drifting probe is easy to tell apart from a real flood.

Motion · PIR

Occupancy

Answers the question that changes everything during an incident: is anyone still in that room right now?

Node health

RSSI · WiFi · Relay · Uptime

A silent sensor is its own kind of alarm. Link quality and packet delay are tracked as first-class signals, not footnotes.

Architecture

Edge to browser, with nothing in between to break.

No server to keep alive, no database to migrate. The dashboard speaks MQTT over WebSockets directly to the broker.

01 · Edge

ESP32 node

Samples every attached sensor on a fixed interval, bundles all rooms into one packet, and stamps it with node ID, RSSI, relay and WiFi state.

ESP32-01
02 · Transport

MQTT broker

A single topic carries the whole campus. Broker host, port, TLS, credentials and topic are all editable from the dashboard settings.

koba-samsu
03 · Command

SCS-RG dashboard

Parses, scores and renders. Room cards build themselves from the payload, so adding a Chemistry Lab tomorrow needs no code change at all.
